# StorySubway models: provenance

Generated by `Tools/Venues/story_subway_people.py` (run from `Tools/Venues/make_story_subway.py`).

The named people on the Bellwoods platform are copies of the game's crowd figure pieces,
`Assets/Models/People/Crowd/<Body>/{Legs,Torso,Head,ArmL,ArmR}.obj`, which `Game/Tools/MakeCrowdPieces.py`
cuts from the owner's Synty POLYGON Town characters (`Assets/Models/People/BodyType1_<Body>.fbx`).
Licence: the owner's Synty licence.

| Files | Body | Clothes atlas | Who |
|-------|------|---------------|-----|
| `Mother_02_02_B_*` | BodyType1_Mother_02 | `Assets/Models/Venue/PolygonTown_02_B.png` | the mustard-scarf commuter (Characters: `commuter`) |
| `Daughter_01_01_A_*` | BodyType1_Daughter_01 | `PolygonTown_01_A.png` | the kid in the yellow coat |
| `Son_01_03_B_*` | BodyType1_Son_01 | `PolygonTown_03_B.png` | the kid with the scooter |
| `Mother_01_04_A_*` | BodyType1_Mother_01 | `PolygonTown_04_A.png` | their mum |

Geometry and UVs are unchanged except arms with a `_<degrees>` suffix, whose forearms are bent
forward at the elbow by that angle (to hold the mug, clap, fold arms). Each `<Body>_<Clothes>.mtl`
binds the Synty Town clothes atlas in `Assets/Models/Venue/` (referenced in place, not copied).

Everything else in the venue (platform, tiles, trains, crates, bucket, case) is engine primitives;
the textures in `../Textures/` are drawn by `Tools/Venues/story_subway_textures.py`.
