# Midnight at the FM Drive In: models

Every OBJ in this folder is original, generated by `Tools/Venues/story_drive_in_meshes.py` (run
`python3 -B Tools/Venues/make_story_drive_in.py` from the repository root). They share
`Palette.mtl`, which maps `../Textures/Palette.png`, a palette of flat colour swatches drawn by
`Tools/Venues/story_drive_in_textures.py`. Sizes are in metres, pivots on the floor, front toward +z
in venue space (the writer mirrors z for Assimp).

| Model | What |
|-------|------|
| `Car<Shape><Paint>.obj` | Low-poly hatchbacks, sedans, wagons, pickups and vans in the lot's paints. `...Open` is the backed-in hatchback with its tailgate up. |
| `SpeakerPost.obj` | Drive-in speaker post with two window speakers. |
| `FMMast.obj` | 15 m triangular lattice FM mast on the booth roof. |
| `Beam.obj` | Unit light-shaft frustum (headlights, projector beam); drawn additive through a part override. |
| `ScreenLattice.obj` | The screen's steel back frame and face boards. |
| `LightTower.obj` | 9 m festival light tower. |
| `Fence.obj` | 40 m post-and-wire lot fence. |
| `Projector.obj` | The booth's projector with its reels. |
| `Thermos.obj` | The thermos between the hatchback and the pickup. |

No Synty files are copied here. The scene also references, read-only and in place, Synty-derived
models under the owner's Synty licence: `Assets/Models/Venue/` (Speaker, DrumRug, FlightCaseWide,
CameraTripod, CableCoil, PowerDistro, AmpRack, RoadCase, Cooler, FloodLight, TourBus, Truck,
FoodStall, FlagA, FlagB; provenance in that folder's README) and `Assets/Venues/Backyard/Models/`
(GardenTree, StreetLamp, and the seated legs `*_Legs_79.obj`), plus the crowd pieces in
`Assets/Models/People/Crowd/`.
