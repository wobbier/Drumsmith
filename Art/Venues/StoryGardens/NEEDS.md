# StoryGardens: framework wishes (none blocking)

- **Cameo gating.** The set's songs have `Cameos` with `If: not_selected` (Rory at the home bench, Kit at the visitors' bench, Dee at a keys rack, and so on). A `VenueStory`-like gate on "member X not selected" would let a venue place those characters. The benches are built and waiting.
- **Cube face UVs.** Textures on the primitive cube come out upside down; the Gardens stores its pictures pre-flipped. A note in `Docs/Venue-Authoring.md` (or a fix) would save the other builders a capture.
- **Rotated crowd areas.** Corner seating has to be approximated with axis-aligned squares.
