# Story venues: the brief for each venue builder

You are building ONE story-mode venue for Drumsmith, a drum rhythm game on MitchEngine (C++20). Twelve other builders are working at the same time on the other venues, in the same checkout. Read this whole file before you start.

## What story mode is

Story mode is 13 five-song sets that take a drummer from a basement to a stadium (`Design/Story-Mode-Setlists.md`, readable; `Design/Story-Mode-Setlists.json`, the manifest). Each set is played at **one venue that changes as the five songs go by**: the dessert table gets pushed to the wall, the pool table gets covered, pledge slips climb the glass. That is the heart of it. The player watches the room turn over across ~20 minutes, mostly in wide and crowd shots behind the note highway.

Your set's manifest entry has everything: `Premise`, `VenueBase`, `VenueWork` (build notes), `KitLook`, `OnStage`, `StateFlags`, and for each song `Cue` (`On`: where in the song, `Shot`: what kind of shot catches it, `Do`: what happens), `StateAfter` (the flags set once that song's cue lands; cumulative). The `Characters` list gives the named people's looks.

## The contract

Read, in this order: `Docs/Venue-Authoring.md` (all of it, especially **VenueStory (story mode)**, units/axes, the markers, budgets, checking and previewing), `Tools/Venues/venuegen.py`, `Tools/Venues/make_template.py`, `Tools/Venues/make_backyard.py` (+ `backyard_*.py`, the most finished venue: how props, models, lights, cameras and accents are done), `Assets/Models/Venue/README.md`.

- Your venue: `Assets/Venues/<Id>/` with `venue.json` (Name = the set's title, Author "Drumsmith", Scene `<Id>.lvl`, Version 1, optional `Preview` jpg, optional `Ambience`) and `<Id>.lvl`, generated by **`Tools/Venues/make_<snake_id>.py`** (deterministic, rerunnable).
- **Every flag in your set's `StateFlags` must make a visible change**, built with `Scene.story(...)` groups (`when_set=True` for things that arrive, `False` for things that leave or move away; a "moved" prop is two groups, one of each). Make the changes big enough to read in a wide shot from the crowd side, and up high or to the sides: the lower-centre of the screen is under the highway. Light changes work too (plain `Light`s under a story group dim with it; a lamp that comes on is a story group holding a Light and an emissive mesh).
- **Band spots = the set's `OnStage`**: `drums` Drummer, `bass` Bass, `guitar` Guitar, `vocals` / `guest-vocals` Vocals, `guest-electronics` / keys Keys, `guest-bass` Bass, `guest-guitar` Guitar. A member with no spot isn't on stage, which is the point in the early sets (the drummer alone in a basement or a subway). Sets 11-13 add a Keys spot. The drum kit is a fixed model about 2.5 m x 2 m in front of the Drummer spot; dress around it for `KitLook` (a bucket and practice pad beside it on the subway, a rug, a tea towel...).
- Crowd: `VenueCrowdArea` rectangles sized to the place (a handful of commuters; a packed bar; 60 000 in a stadium via big Mob areas plus Posed front rows). Crowd areas, spots and cameras can't be story-gated.
- Cameras: a `Wide` that frames the room and the story changes, a `Crane`, a `Desk`, and `Custom`/`Close` shots aimed at where the story beats happen. An `arrival/01..` intro walk is a nice touch.
- Lights: at most ONE shadow-casting light (directional), at most 40 `VenueShowLight`s. Never put a `VenueShowLight` under a story group. Intensities are engine units (see the docs; calibrate from the Template/Backyard and your previews).
- Look: low-poly Synty style, like the Backyard. Use primitives (`box`, `slab`), models from `Assets/Models/Venue/` and `Assets/Venues/Backyard/Models/` (reference them in place, read-only), and the owner's Synty FBX packs in `/home/mitch/Projects/MitchGame/Assets/Models/Synty/` (ExplorerKit, OfficePack, Town): **copy** any you use into `Assets/Venues/<Id>/Models/` with their textures and add a provenance `Assets/Venues/<Id>/Models/README.md` (pack, file, licence "owner's Synty licence"). You may draw textures (ImageMagick `magick` is on the PATH; see `Tools/Hideout/make_textures.py`) into your own folder: signage, posters, the chalkboard, the departure board. No real brands, logos or likenesses (a real venue *name* the design uses, like Maple Leaf Gardens or the Legion, is fine as text).
- Ambience (optional): a looping crowd/room sound in your folder (ffmpeg can synthesise; `Tools/Hideout/make_sounds.py`) or `Assets/Sounds/crowd.mp3`.

## Rules (shared checkout: these keep 13 builders out of each other's way)

- **Write only** `Assets/Venues/<Id>/`, `Tools/Venues/make_<snake_id>.py` (plus helper modules named `Tools/Venues/<snake_id>_*.py`), and `Art/Venues/<Id>/` (notes, previews, scratch). Never edit anything else: not `venuegen.py`, not `Game/`, `Engine/`, `Docs/`, `Design/`, other venues, `Assets/Models/`, `Assets/Progression/`, `Settings.json`.
- **Do not build** the game or editor, and do not run `Tools/Story/*.py`. The binary `./.build/Game_linux_Debug/Game_EntryPoint_x64` is current. If you need a framework change, stop working around it and write it in `Art/Venues/<Id>/NEEDS.md`.
- **No git** writes: no commit, add, stash, reset, checkout.
- **Headless captures share one machine (8 cores, 15 GB).** Every game run MUST go through the lock, and use your own display number:

```bash
flock /tmp/drumsmith-capture.lock env HEADLESS_DISPLAY=:<your display> ./Engine/Tools/NixEnv.sh env -u NIX_CC \
  ./Engine/Tools/Headless.sh ./.build/Game_linux_Debug/Game_EntryPoint_x64 \
  --scene Assets/Example.lvl --play-track 0 --frames 60 --frame-time 0.016666667 --no-audio \
  --venue-only --venue <Id> --venue-time 40 --story <set id> --story-song N [--story-cue-at 0] \
  [--venue-eye "x,y,z,tx,ty,tz,fov"] [--venue-shot <name>] --screenshot Art/Venues/<Id>/Previews/<name>.png --exit
```

  Keep runs short (60 frames). Wrap with `timeout 600`. Look at every image you take (Read the PNG). Budget roughly 15-25 captures; don't hold the lock while thinking.
- `python3 Tools/Venues/check_venue.py Assets/Venues/<Id>` must pass with no errors.

6. One more of your choosing that shows off the place.

## Done means

`check_venue.py` passes; the room reads as the place in the design in a single wide; every StateFlag visibly changes the room; the band spots match OnStage; the six previews exist and you've looked at them and fixed what was wrong. Then write `Art/Venues/<Id>/REPORT.md`: files made, what each flag does, cameras, light count, what you couldn't do and why, and anything for `NEEDS.md`. Your final message: a 5-10 line summary with the preview paths.

Quality bar: this should look like a place a player remembers. Iterate on the images until it does.
